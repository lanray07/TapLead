## App and accounts

TapLead supports local digital cards, professional connections, notes and follow-up dates. Demo content is clearly labelled. Public profiles and account synchronisation require a configured live service. Only publish or share information and images you are authorised to use; obtain appropriate permission before recording or submitting another person's details. Avoid publishing confidential information or using the service for spam, impersonation or unlawful activity.

Optional AI, Teams and Wallet services are not offered as verified production features. Network-dependent features may be unavailable while a service is unconfigured or offline. See the [support guide](https://github.com/lanray07/TapLead/blob/main/docs/SUPPORT.md) and [privacy policy](https://github.com/lanray07/TapLead/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY.md).

## TapLead Pro subscriptions

Monthly and Annual provide the same Pro service level: up to 20 published card personas and 10,000 synced leads. Their configured product identifiers are `com.taplead.pro.monthly` and `com.taplead.pro.yearly`. The annual product bills for one year upfront. Current draft UK base prices are £4.99 per month and £39.99 per year, with no introductory trial; local pricing is shown by Apple before an actual purchase. Purchases remain unavailable until production configuration and Apple Sandbox verification are complete.

When purchasing is enabled, payment is handled by Apple and charged to your Apple account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account settings. TapLead includes Restore purchases in Settings → TapLead Pro. Deleting your TapLead account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Refund requests are handled through [Apple's refund service](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/), subject to Apple's process and applicable rights.
