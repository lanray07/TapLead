# TapLead support

TapLead helps you create a digital business card, organise connections and plan follow-ups.

## Getting started

Open TapLead and follow onboarding. The demo option lets you explore with clearly labelled sample connections. Open **My card** to edit your profile. Open **Connections** to add a connection, save meeting notes or set a follow-up. Your upcoming follow-ups appear in **Today**.

Live profile publication and synced connections require a configured account service. Pro purchases are unavailable until the production service and Apple Sandbox verification are complete.

## Add a logo or photo to a business card

Open **My card → Edit card → Card image**. Choose **Photo** or **Logo**, then choose an image from Photos or import an image file. Choose **Top left**, **Top right**, **Bottom left** or **Bottom right** for its position. The preview updates before you save. Only one image is displayed on a card; logos keep their full shape and photos fill a rounded frame.

Use **Remove image** to clear the selected image, or **None** to hide the corner image. Images up to 20 MB are accepted and reduced to a thumbnail for local storage. Wait for loading to finish before saving. Cancel discards changes. These images remain on your iPhone; public profile image hosting is not enabled yet.

## Subscription help

TapLead Pro Monthly and Annual provide the same service level. Apple handles billing. Manage or cancel an active subscription in your Apple account's subscription settings. In TapLead, **Settings → TapLead Pro → Restore purchases** starts purchase restoration.

## Report a problem or ask a question

[Open a TapLead support issue](https://github.com/lanray07/TapLead/issues/new). Include the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and steps that reproduce the problem. Describe what you expected and what happened.

GitHub issues are public. Omit passwords, billing details and private contact information. Use fictional connection details in any screenshots you attach.
